The ease of Notes. The freedom of files.

Notebook keeps every note as a plain Markdown file, in your own iCloud Drive or any folder you choose. There is no vault to set up and nothing to import: your notes sit right next to the PDFs, photos and documents they belong with, and they stay yours. Obsidian and other Markdown apps open the same files.

And it writes like Notes. Headings, bold, lists and checklists look finished as you type, and their Markdown marks show only on the line you're writing. Pictures, PDFs and drawings go right into the note. On the Mac, iPhone and iPad it's an Apple app through and through: widgets, Spotlight, Shortcuts, the share sheet, Apple Pencil and the keyboard shortcuts you know.

NEW IN 1.3
• Tables you type in as in Notes, with handles to add, align and delete rows and columns
• AsciiDoc notes, right next to your Markdown ones
• Quick Look and thumbnails show a note as Notebook does, in the Finder and the Files app
• Paste a Markdown file, and its heading becomes the note's title
• The toolbar and the first screen laid out as in Notes, and a new icon

WRITE
• Tables, code, maths and Mermaid diagrams, drawn right in the note
• Handwritten notes on lined, squared, dotted or Cornell paper, with Apple Pencil or a finger, kept as PDFs
• Pictures, PDFs and files in the note, kept in a hidden .assets folder beside it
• Drawings with Apple Pencil, a finger, the mouse or the trackpad, in the note and right on PDFs
• Version history: every earlier version of a note, with what changed marked
• [[Links]] between notes that keep working when you rename or move them

FIND
• Search finds words in notes, PDFs, pictures, scans and handwriting, and names from a few letters, typos included
• ⌘K finds anything and runs any command, and every note is in Spotlight

TO-DOS
• Every open checkbox of every note in one list, by when it's due, as in Reminders
• Dates, times, places and repeats are written into the note, where other Markdown apps read them too

YOUR FOLDERS
• iCloud Drive as the Finder shows it, with drag and drop, and Undo for moves, renames and the Trash
• Share to Notebook from any app, widgets for a new note, today's to-dos and a note, and import from Apple Notes
• Folder symbols and colours, kept where the Finder keeps them

PRIVATE
• No account, no server, no tracking. Notebook sees only the folders you choose.

To look around first, Try Sample Notes on the first screen makes a folder of sample notes.
