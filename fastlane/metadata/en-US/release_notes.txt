Notebook 1.2 brings handwritten notes.

• Handwritten notes on iPhone and iPad: write with Apple Pencil or a finger on blank, lined, squared, dotted or Cornell paper. A handwritten note is a PDF in your folder, right next to your typed notes, and every PDF app shows what you wrote. On the iPad, New Note asks whether to type or write by hand.
• Pages as you need them: pull past the last page for a new one, give a page other paper, and see every page at once in All Pages, where you drag pages into a new order, duplicate and delete them.
• Pictures on the page: photos, the camera, scans and pictures from Files, moved, turned and resized with the lasso, and as sharp as the handwriting however far you zoom in.
• All of it works on any PDF, so you can write next to lecture slides and after the last one.
• On the Mac: draw on PDFs with the mouse, the trackpad or a tablet, highlight their text with ⌃⌘H, and hold Shift for a straight line.
• VoiceOver reads a note as you see it, line by line, and a double-tap ticks a to-do.
• Nothing you write is lost: a note that can't be saved is tried again and kept in Unsaved Notes, and where there is no Recently Deleted, Undo asks before deleting a note you have written in.
