Notebook 1.3 brings tables you type in as in Notes.

• Tables stay tables while you type: the cell you're in has a ring, and handles on its row and column add, align and delete rows and columns. Return adds a row, Tab goes to the next cell, and the file stays plain Markdown. Table in the toolbar puts a new one in.
• AsciiDoc notes: an .adoc file is a note like a Markdown one, shown as it reads while you type, with its to-dos in To-dos and its headings in search.
• Quick Look shows a note as Notebook does: press Space on a note in the Finder, or tap one in the Files app, and you see its headings, to-dos, tables and maths instead of its Markdown. Thumbnails show notes like that too.
• Paste a whole Markdown file and its # Heading becomes the note's title. On the Mac, ⌘V in the sidebar pastes files and text into a folder, as in the Finder.
• A look like Apple's own apps: the toolbar laid out as in Notes, To-dos and Favorites as tiles at the top, with the files you opened last as cards, and an icon that fills its square.
• On the Mac, Search is at the top of the sidebar and opens ⌘K, menus show their symbols and find an entry as you type, and a caret shows where what you drag will go.
• Add Location is now Open Folder.
• Notebook is about half the size to download, typing stays light in long notes and next to pictures and tables, and notes with long tables open several times faster.
• Fixed: lines you write at the bottom stay above the keyboard on iPhone and iPad, emoji and Chinese or Japanese characters show as soon as a note opens, and adding a note on the Mac can no longer make Notebook hang.
