Notebook is a notes app for the Markdown files in folders the user chooses. It has no accounts and no server (Guideline 4.8 does not apply).

To review without notes of your own, choose "Try Sample Notes" on the first screen ("Try Sample Notes…" on the Mac). On the Mac it asks where to put them (Documents, say) and makes a folder Sample Notes there; on iPhone and iPad it makes it in the app's own folder, which the Files app shows under On My iPhone › Notebook. The folder then shows in the sidebar, with notes, pictures, a drawing, PDFs and to-dos. Every feature works there. To use your own files instead, choose "Open iCloud Drive" or "Choose another folder…".

The Mac app runs in the App Sandbox and reaches only the folders the user chose, through security-scoped bookmarks.

Network: Notebook connects to nothing of its own. Searching for a place for a reminder or a to-do uses Apple Maps (MapKit), with the words typed, and Current Location asks MapKit for the address.

iCloud: the folders the user hides and the reminders they set are kept in iCloud's key-value store of the user's own Apple account, so their devices agree. Notes in iCloud Drive are synced by iCloud Drive itself. Notebook's own iCloud container (iCloud.com.lukaskbl.Notebook, not shown in iCloud Drive) holds the earlier versions of notes in iCloud, for version history (File › Revert To › Browse All Versions… on the Mac, Version History in a note's ⋯ menu on iPhone and iPad), and To-dos.md, the to-dos added in To-dos without a note. Nothing goes to the developer.

Import from Apple Notes (File › Import from Apple Notes… on the Mac, the ⋯ menu on iPhone and iPad) reads the Markdown that Notes itself exports (File › Export as › Markdown in Notes); it doesn't read Notes' data.

Permissions, each asked for only when first needed:
• Notifications: when the user sets the first reminder of a note or a to-do with Remind Me…, for instance on a to-do in Inbox › Errands of the sample notes. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; to-dos with a time remind only once notifications are allowed.
• Location (While Using only): when the user gives a reminder a place, or chooses Current Location. On iPhone and iPad the place is handed to the system as a region to watch; the app keeps no record of where the user is.
• Camera (iPhone and iPad): when the user takes a photo or scans a document into a note from the bar above the keyboard.
• Contacts: none. Home and Work, as a reminder's place, can be picked from the user's own card with the system's contact picker, which gives the app only the address tapped.

Widgets and controls: New Note is a widget (Home Screen, Lock Screen, the Mac's desktop) and a control (Control Center, Lock Screen, Action button), which open Notebook with a new note in the default folder. The Today widget shows the to-dos due today and the overdue ones (try it on the sample notes), and its circles tick them; the Note widget shows a note picked under Edit Widget. The app keeps what these two show in its App Group, on the device.

Share extension: "Notebook" in the share sheet of other apps (try a page in Safari or a photo in Photos) takes text, links, pictures and PDFs into a new note in the default folder or at the end of the note opened last. The extension leaves them in the app's App Group, and the app writes them into the user's folder the next time it is open.

Spotlight: the notes in the user's folders are indexed on the device with Core Spotlight, so system search finds them; notes in hidden folders are left out.

On iPhone and iPad, background refresh lets reminders set on another device be scheduled while the app is closed.
